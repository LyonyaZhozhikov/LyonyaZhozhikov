### :test_tube: Research

I study hereditary diseases of the Yakut population, combining clinical genetics, structural modelling and molecular diagnostics.

- **SOPH syndrome (NBAS)** — clinical spectrum of 93 Yakut patients, SOPH-associated immunodeficiency, and AlphaFold-based subdomain map of the NBAS protein and its interaction sites
- **Protein–variant–phenotype approach** — using AlphaFold / AlphaFold-Multimer to explain why variants in different protein regions lead to different phenotypes (NBAS, EXT1/EXT2)
- **Molecular diagnostics** — developed a real-time PCR test with allele-specific probes for NBAS c.5741G>A (RU patent 2819985), implemented at the NEFU Clinic since 2024
- **Multiple osteochondromas (EXT1/EXT2)** and **nutrigenomics** of Arctic children
